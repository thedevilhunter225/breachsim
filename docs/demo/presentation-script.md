# FYP Demo Script

1. Log in as the demo admin and open the executive dashboard.
2. Import synthetic employees from CSV or inspect seeded directory data.
3. Show each employee's profile data and approved-context profile.
4. Open organization policy settings and explain allowed themes, channels, and guardrails.
5. Generate a finance-themed sandbox email scenario for a selected group.
6. Review the generated content, highlight rationale metadata, then approve it as admin.
7. Launch the campaign in sandbox mode and open the delivery log.
8. Click a training token from the sample report to show the safe landing page.
9. Trigger a tracked interaction and show the immediate micro-training card.
10. Refresh analytics to show updated events, failure reasons, and risk score changes.
11. Open department insights and compare vulnerability and improvement trends.
12. Export an audit report and show the approval history and append-only log.
