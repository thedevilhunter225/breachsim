**Suggested subject:** Our security awareness programme starts on [Programme start date]

## About the programme

From [Programme start date], [Customer legal name] is running a security awareness programme to help all of us recognise and report phishing. It uses BreachSim, a service provided by [Company legal name].

During the programme you may receive simulated phishing emails, some containing a QR code, sent from our own email system. They are realistic on purpose, so you will not be told in advance. If you follow a link or scan a code, you will see a short training page that explains the warning signs you could have spotted. No text-message, phone-call or deepfake (cloned voice or video) simulations are used.

## What is measured

- Whether our email system accepted or bounced each simulated message.
- Whether you opened the link or scanned the QR code, and when.
- The option you chose on the training page — for example "Report suspicious", "Verify independently" or "I would provide details" — and when.
- Training assigned to you, whether you completed it and how long you spent on it.
- A risk score and trend calculated from the above.

Whether you open a simulated email is not tracked, and if you never follow the link or scan the code, nothing else is recorded. The training pages never ask for, collect or store passwords, verification codes or payment details. Your IP address and device details are not stored with your results. Your name, work email, department and role, as provided by [Customer legal name], are used to address and personalise the messages.

## Your choices

- **Opt out.** After you respond to a simulation, the training page links to your employee portal ("Your training and privacy options"). There you can opt out of future simulations at any time.
- **Ask for your data to be deleted.** From the same portal you can ask us to delete your exercise data. This also stops further simulations, and our administrators will act on the request within [Response time].
- **Contact us.** If you no longer have the link, or want to use any other right you have under data protection law — such as access, correction or objection — contact [Customer privacy contact].

The portal link works like a key to your portal, so please do not forward it. It stops working when the simulation link expires (30 days by default).
